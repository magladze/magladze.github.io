Capabilities

Three disciplines, one delivery.

Most AI work fails at the seams — between the model, the data underneath it, and the operational reality it has to survive. I work across all three.

AI architecture

Retrieval-augmented generation, agentic automation and workflow copilots, designed around a client's actual data, tools and constraints — not a reference diagram. Document intelligence at scale where it genuinely moves the needle.

  • RAG & semantic retrieval
  • Agent runtimes & tool orchestration
  • Evaluation harnesses for quality, latency and cost
  • Guardrails, offline benchmarks, online A/B

Data architecture

The layer everything else stands on. Modeling, warehousing and large-scale ETL that hold up under production load, audit and change — across cloud platforms and legacy systems that still have to be carried forward.

  • Warehouse & lakehouse design

  • SQL and NoSQL modeling
  • Master data management

Delivery & MLOps

Systems that outlive the engagement. Infrastructure as code, CI/CD for models and prompts, observability wired to SLAs, cost governance, and the runbooks a client's own team needs to operate what they've been handed.

Selected work

Shipped, not slideware.

A sample of recent engagements. Client names are withheld where the work is under contract; details are described at the level of the architecture.

  1. 2026 Principal AI Architect & Engineer

    Intelligent document processing for an enterprise insurance carrier

    An end-to-end IDP system taking scanned policyholder forms through a multi-stage pipeline: deterministic catalog matching first, OCR second, and LLM classification only as fallback. The tiered cascade was designed as a reusable pattern the client could redeploy across adjacent document workflows rather than a single-purpose build.

    Delivered solo as roughly 16,000 lines of production Python and Terraform — eleven Lambda functions, an agent runtime container, serverless Postgres, queues with dead-letter handling, and observability across the pipeline — alongside thirteen operational and architectural documents so the client's own DevOps team could take it from there.

    • Textract
    • Terraform
    • Lambda
    • Aurora Serverless v2
    • SQS / DLQ
    • Python
  2. 2022 — 2024 Data Engineer Architect & Lead Python Developer

    Federal healthcare document migration and workflow rebuild

    Lead developer on migrating unstructured documents off a legacy IBM FileNet estate into AWS, and on rebuilding an existing Camunda-based vetting workflow natively on the AWS side — both as custom multi-step architectures rather than lift-and-shift.

    Also maintained and extended a Python ETL framework, and built a generic SQL-interfaced ETL tool that let two data teams run their own pipelines without needing the backend knowledge the previous process demanded. Recognized with the organization's Star Performer award.

    • AWS Athena
    • RDS
    • IBM Db2
    • NiFi
    • Jenkins
    • Python

About

An unusual route to the work.

I read Political Science at Cornell, then spent the next decade going the other direction entirely — from analytics and software engineering into data architecture, and from there into the applied AI work I do now. The detour turned out to be useful: most of what makes these projects succeed or fail is not the model.

In 2023 I founded IntelliSoft Ventures to do this work directly with clients — custom AI implementations taken from discovery and acceptance testing through pilot and into production, tied to outcomes that can actually be measured. Before that I architected data and AI systems in federal healthcare, cloud consulting and product engineering.

I write, too. Ten-plus titles on the libraries and systems I build with, because teaching something is the fastest way to find out how well you understand it.

Based in Las Vegas, Nevada. Working with clients anywhere.
